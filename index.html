Before you book

Answers to what guests usually ask. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

Check dates
What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 1:00 pm and check-out is by 11:00 am. Early check-in or late check-out can usually be arranged if the house is free the day before or after.

Is the whole villa ours, or is it shared?

The whole villa is yours. All three bedrooms, the sunroom, the garden and the pool are only for your group.

Can we bring more than 10 people?

Ten is the limit for an overnight stay. Day visitors can come by if you tell us in advance, up to 4 extra people.

Is food available?

Our caretaker cooks Maharashtrian vegetarian and non-vegetarian thalis on request, charged per plate. You are also welcome to order in or bring your own food.

How deep is the pool? Is it safe for children?

It is a round plunge pool about 4 ft deep. It is not for diving. Children must be with an adult in and around the pool at all times.

Are pets allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome with a small cleaning fee. Please keep them out of the pool and off the beds.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 7 days before check-in. 50% refund between 7 and 3 days. No refund within 3 days of arrival.

How do we get there?

Karjat is about 2 hours from Mumbai by road. If you come by train, we can arrange a pickup from Karjat station. The exact location pin is shared after booking.
